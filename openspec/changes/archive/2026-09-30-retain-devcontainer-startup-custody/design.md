# Fixed startup operation

The existing trust/selection/policy gates remain in `prepare_sandbox_env`. Its small `prepare_devcontainer` helper is the shipping startup/publication/fallback decision. A private controller reserves one entry before owning provider inputs or creating/probing the provider. The installed owner alone calls preparation and spawn; a final PENDING→SPAWNING claim follows allocation and trust verification. Caller cancellation cannot be overwritten by spawn completion.

One retained owner waits on its exclusively owned Child while one reader captures into a fixed 2-MiB+1 byte buffer (the extra byte detects overflow). Stdout remains null. Reader/setup/read/wait failures, post-spawn panic, cancellation, deadline and nonzero status all prevent fallback and reuse. No startup kill/PGID/taskkill policy is introduced; a held helper may continue running. Owned OS/filesystem calls can remain blocked, but the caller uses the original checked deadline and never joins these workers.

Nonzero status first marks a nonreusable HELD_DRAINING disposition. The retained owner may join the bounded reader to preserve the existing first-line stderr diagnostic; it publishes the nonzero error after EOF. Descendant-held stderr instead reaches the unchanged caller deadline and remains held. This diagnostic-order refinement was explicitly approved by primary review; it is not prompt process termination or a reset deadline. Diagnostic display retains at most 512 bytes plus exit code; no complete output/config/environment/panic payload is logged.

A non-Clone PendingSession publishes through the real session registry before acknowledgement. Its pre-admitted retired-cell guard receives `map.insert`'s displaced session directly, before either guard releases; an immediate post-insertion unwind retains old custody. Provider/snapshot cleanup occurs on the owner, never the publisher. Actual owner completion, not an acknowledgement message, gates reusable admission. The exact controller entry remains retained after ambiguous outcomes, abandoned successes or unsafe cleanup; it does not infer absence of a container from child exit.

The startup lane does not share Git/capability budgets, readers or reapers. Tests use private controllers, actual inert CLI commands, shortened deadlines at the same production worker and owned IO fault/barrier hooks. The actual agent helper test uses a private controller/registry override and inert continuation counter, paired with the production `?` early-return source; it does not claim full sandbox/trust-DB invocation. Platform-native process proof remains separate from cross-compilation.

Fixture cleanup retains actual Arc<TempDir> leases, including migrated direct-up tests; it observes unfinished handles/resources in place. An unsettled cleanup reports failure and retains a fixed test-only operation/directory reference; an assertion unwind never double-panics or deletes live fixture paths. This emergency test retention is deliberately absent from production code and can be cleared only after exact owned settlement.
