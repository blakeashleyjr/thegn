## Finding 1: graceful daemon shutdown does not own/drain live actors

Confirmed on this branch:

1. `crates/thegn-host/src/daemon/service.rs:1970-1973`: `ControlApi::shutdown` only calls `self.shutdown.notify_waiters()`.
2. `crates/thegn-host/src/daemon/mod.rs:519-525`: the main Axum server waits for that notification, then returns. The post-serve cleanup at `527-536` removes daemon registry/lease rows and the socket; it does not stop sessions first. TCP serving at `463-468` also uses notification-based graceful stop.
3. `crates/thegn-host/src/daemon/fork.rs:100-111`: `spawn_session` inserts a `SessionEntry`, then detaches the actor task with `tokio::spawn`; no actor `JoinHandle` is retained in the entry or service.
4. `crates/thegn-host/src/daemon/session.rs:382-440`: an actor exits on `SessionMsg::Kill` or closed mailbox. Its teardown signals only the direct PID at `450-452`, then finalizes recording at `454-458`, writes tombstone/removes session at `460-490`, and performs remaining bookkeeping through `505+`.
5. `crates/thegn-host/src/daemon/mod.rs:272-284` claims daemon PTYs are handled by fd-close/SIGHUP (and bwrap namespace collapse) on daemon death. This is not an explicit graceful shutdown protocol and does not meet bounded TERM/drain/KILL, verified survivor cleanup, or deterministic actor joining. It also does not establish descendants outside bwrap are terminated.

**Failure cases:** shutdown RPC/SIGTERM stops listeners and removes daemon metadata while live actors are still running; child/grandchild can retain PTY/resource handles or continue modifying a worktree. A shutdown racing session creation can also admit a new session after a one-time drain unless admission is closed and synchronized with the session registry.

## Finding 2: numeric PID/PGID signaling races with reaping

Confirmed on this branch; the captured line references are stale but the ordering remains:

- `crates/thegn-host/src/pane.rs:212-224`: `PtyPane::drop` loads `child_reaped` and, if false, constructs `GroupHandle::from_pid(pid)` and signals after the load.
- `crates/thegn-host/src/pane_pty.rs:163-170`: the reader calls `child.wait()` first (which releases the child PID), then publishes `reaped = true`. A concurrent Drop can observe false after `wait()` released the PID and signal a recycled numeric process-group ID. Atomic ordering cannot close the gap between OS reaping and the later flag store.
- `crates/thegn-host/src/platform/unix.rs:432-461`: `GroupHandle` stores only numeric `pgid`; `terminate`/`kill` call `killpg` with it. No stable process identity or synchronization with the reader's wait exists.
- `crates/thegn-host/src/daemon/session.rs:442-452`: actor Kill/Exit has the same race in another form: a queued Kill can win while reader Exit is pending, and actor signals the stored numeric PID based on `child_exited`, not a synchronized child ownership guard.

**Failure cases:** pane Drop or daemon Kill arrives after the reader has reaped the leader but before its atomic is visible / Exit is consumed, so a signal may hit an unrelated process/group after PID reuse. Conversely, a child trapping TERM survives the current best-effort TERM with no bounded KILL escalation or survivor verification.

## Smallest complete fix proposal

Keep the change in the existing PTY/daemon lifecycle seam; do not add a new user-facing feature. Introduce one shared per-child lifecycle owner created with the child before its reaper starts. It must own the `Child` plus platform process-tree handle and arbitrate natural-exit observation/reaping against shutdown signaling. Replace the atomic check plus raw numeric signal path in ordinary `PtyPane::drop` and daemon actor teardown with this same owner. On Unix, never let an uncoordinated `wait()` release the group leader identity while a signal can still be issued; use a nonblocking/polling reaper coordinated under the owner lock or an equivalent stable identity mechanism. Keep Windows Job Object behavior within `src/platform/windows.rs` and the shared API in `src/platform/`.

For session/daemon shutdown, add an explicit service shutdown coordinator: atomically stop admitting session creation, stop listener acceptance, snapshot and signal every currently owned session, and await each actor/child completion under one bounded grace policy. On timeout, verify liveness through the owned identity and force-kill survivors; do not signal a bare stale PID. Retain actor join/termination receipts rather than detaching tasks. Preserve deterministic teardown: terminate/reap process tree; settle reader/writer and close PTY pipes; finalize recording; publish tombstone/remove session and leases; then remove daemon registry/socket state. Kill/Exit races must be idempotent through the lifecycle owner.

Persist a minimal daemon/session ownership lifecycle record for diagnostics (daemon generation, session ID, identity/phase, and shutdown result), and mark/retire it as teardown completes. Treat records from a dead daemon as diagnostics only: do not use a persisted numeric PID to signal a potentially re-parented or reused process. Current `daemons` rows and session lease rows (`crates/thegn-core/src/db_control.rs`) do not persist the live session child/process-group identity or shutdown outcome. If primary review interprets “persist enough state” as requiring automatic crash-time signaling of descendants, that conflicts with the explicit no-reparented-process targeting constraint and needs a separate design decision; the safe proposal is diagnostics-only crash recovery.
