## Security review and correction

- The original decision overstated pipeline-artifact provenance. The current
  completion gate proves that the artifact path exists as a regular file and is
  present in git's index; it deliberately treats a dirty worktree as
  non-blocking. A newly staged artifact or tracked artifact modified after its
  commit can therefore pass even though the bytes a reviewer reads are not in
  `HEAD`. Commit `cdd345e8` corrects the decision record and requires any future
  memory indexer to read pipeline text from a named commit rather than the
  mutable index/worktree.
- The original session-summary row omitted a privacy boundary. An unfiltered
  `agent.sessions` call scans every configured harness store, and the summary is
  first-user-prompt content that may be sensitive even though credential
  material and transcript bodies are excluded. Commit `cdd345e8` documents the
  exact-worktree filter requirement and the content sensitivity.
- No product code, config, schema, capability, help, provider, platform, MCP,
  daemon, or ratchet file changed. Direct source review found control-character
  stripping and length caps on reports/notes, regular-file rejection for final
  artifact symlinks, sanitized generated artifact paths, and explicit
  profile/workspace isolation requirements for any future search capability.
