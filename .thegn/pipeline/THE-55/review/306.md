## Blocking finding

1. **The source coldness/owner checks are TOCTOU probes, not guards held for
   the migration.** `with_source_instance_guard` calls
   `profile::instance_running_at` and immediately releases the lock obtained by
   the successful probe before opening either DB
   (`crates/thegn-host/src/cmd/session_move.rs:240-247`,
   `crates/thegn-core/src/profile.rs:340-357,394-400`). Interactive startup does
   not acquire its long-lived singleton until after subcommand dispatch
   (`crates/thegn-host/src/main.rs:855-895,908-925`), so a compositor can start
   after the probe and before target commit/source cleanup. Likewise, source
   daemon sessions are listed once (or re-listed once after `--kill`) and no
   daemon-side fence prevents `session open` from creating a matching live
   session after that listing (`crates/thegn-host/src/cmd/session_move.rs:175-221,359-385`).
   The new source snapshot comparison at cleanup catches DB changes that have
   already persisted, but it cannot catch a newly launched live session or a
   compositor that starts/persists after the comparison. The command can thus
   report `source_deleted=true` while the source is no longer cold, and a late
   compositor persist can recreate the moved source presentation. Required
   next step: introduce a migration-exclusive source guard held across the
   entire operation and respected by compositor startup, plus a daemon-side
   migration fence/lease (or equivalent atomic refusal of new matching session
   opens) through cleanup. Add deterministic race tests that pause after the
   initial owner/session listings and attempt both starts.
