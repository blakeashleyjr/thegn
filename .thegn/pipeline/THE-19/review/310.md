## Findings and review fixes

The row-303 fixes for bounded hook-pipe completion, fallible lifecycle worker
spawns, and session-end/destroy serialization are sound under the scoped
regressions. The environment firewall still clears inherited state, excludes
credential-shaped variables and inherited `THEGN_*` values, and installs only
the typed lifecycle context. Repo hook approvals remain content-addressed and
repo hooks remain warn-only after approval. Hook output/log bounds,
redaction, owner-only Unix permissions, blocking create rollback, timeout
handling, and env/config ratchets remain intact.

This review found and fixed two remaining destructive-workspace failure races:

1. A workspace deletion with a mixture of already-claimed and free worktree
   paths silently discarded the overlapping paths, then could report the whole
   workspace removed even if the other deletion later failed. Overlaps now
   remain explicit failures while independent paths continue.
2. Destroy claims were released by workers before their completion reached the
   compositor, leaving stale groups briefly actionable for duplicate hooks and
   physical removal. Claims now remain held until the typed completion is
   consumed. SQLite open/query failures also no longer collapse to an empty
   candidate list and false success; known live paths may still be processed,
   but the workspace is retained for a safe retry.

Fix commit: `87e6240d fix(the-19): close destroy claim failure races`.
