## Acceptance review anchors

- URL parsing and webcal normalization are performed in configuration and again
  at backend startup (`crates/thegn-core/src/config_calendar.rs:495-563`,
  `crates/thegn-svc/src/calendar/ics_url.rs:43-55`,
  `crates/thegn-svc/src/calendar/caldav.rs:47-59`).
- Automatic redirects and ambient proxies are disabled; automatic content
  decompression is disabled; connect, read-idle, pool-idle, and operation
  deadlines are distinct (`crates/thegn-svc/src/http.rs:154-175`,
  `crates/thegn-svc/src/http.rs:106-123`,
  `crates/thegn-svc/src/http.rs:470-563`).
- DNS answers are capped and all checked addresses must satisfy the same policy
  before reqwest receives them (`crates/thegn-svc/src/http.rs:186-241,
302-358`). The two process-wide clients are split by the trusted private
  destination policy and keep auth/validators request-local
  (`crates/thegn-svc/src/http.rs:64-104,126-175`).
- ICS and CalDAV use the shared client on every fetch, reject all redirect
  statuses before inspecting/following `Location`, and never replay the CalDAV
  sync-token body (`crates/thegn-svc/src/calendar/ics_url.rs:108-168`,
  `crates/thegn-svc/src/calendar/caldav.rs:335-398`).
- Errors are mapped to static/redacted diagnostics; the only logged CalDAV
  status is the numeric status field, not a URL, Location, token, body, or
  credential (`crates/thegn-svc/src/calendar/mod.rs:43-57`,
  `crates/thegn-host/src/hydrate_calendar.rs:195-205`).
