## Findings fixed

Commit `c300c51e` fixes two security/failure-path gaps found during this review:

1. Archive validation accepted required filenames without proving they were
   regular files. A tar member such as root `thegn` could be a symlink, hardlink,
   device, or other special entry and still pass before the workflow extracted
   the archive. Validation now rejects non-regular members and ambiguous
   duplicate normalized paths, and verifies every required root entry is a
   regular file.
2. `atomic_write_output` resolved the requested output path before checking
   whether it was a symlink, making its later symlink rejection unreachable for
   a direct link. It now rejects a symlink before resolution and preserves the
   caller-owned target. Regression tests cover both failure families.

No blocking findings remain after those fixes.
