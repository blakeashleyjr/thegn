A failure to spawn, poll, signal, reap, or settle is also infrastructure `Error`, never `Failed` or `Passed`. A descendant that deliberately escapes the process group/job is likewise outside the containment guarantee.

## Failure cases the fix and regression coverage must distinguish

- Huge simultaneous stdout and stderr (memory bounded by configured reader tails, not total emitted bytes); split multi-byte UTF-8 boundaries (lossy conversion only after retaining bytes); ordinary exit 0; ordinary nonzero gate exit remains candidate `Failed`; nonzero setup exit remains infrastructure `Error`.
- Missing executable/spawn error, reader I/O error/panic, child poll/reaper error, and inability to establish process-group/job ownership: infrastructure `Error`, never a pass or candidate blame.
- Endless writer; deadline on setup and separately on gate; child exits while same-group grandchild holds either/both pipes; ignored graceful termination requiring force kill; direct caller cancellation if a supported token exists. Assert both output buffers remain capped, command eventually settles where OS termination permits, no detached reader is left, and workspace lease is not released/reused before all child and reader ownership settles.
- A direct shell exiting zero while a same-group grandchild keeps pipes open must not yield transient `Passed` or cleanup/reuse. A timeout or transport failure must not enter `bisect_offender`.
- No test may signal an unrelated/reused process; include a fixture proving the target process/group identity remains owned until signaling and reaping.
- Document that uninterruptible filesystem I/O can exceed deadlines because the OS may not schedule the process for termination; do not claim a universal upper bound on cancellation completion.
