## Findings

### F-04 — revisions-needed, medium: vanished worktree batches leak inflight reservations

Location: `crates/thegn-host/src/handlers/provision.rs:447-450`.

`drain_specs` now performs the stable-target validation before settling the
request reservation. When the worktree group has been removed before the
worker result arrives, the `position` lookup fails and the function `continue`s
without removing either `prewarm_inflight` or `materialize_inflight` for the
captured `(group, tab)` key. Each removed group with a late result can leave a
set entry behind; repeated worktree churn therefore grows loop-local state.
Recreating the same group name can also inherit the stale key and suppress
future prewarm dispatch for that tab.

The regression test at
`crates/thegn-host/src/handlers/provision.rs:1419-1487`,
`drain_specs_releases_prewarm_reservation_when_group_disappears`, reproduces a
late `PrewarmSkipped` result after the group is gone and requires the
reservation to be released without dirtying or relayout. It is intentionally
expected to fail against the current implementation until the owner settles
the vanished-group reservation in a way that cannot touch a replacement group.

## Follow-up corrections verified in source

- `SpecBatch.target_leaves` is carried by both the prewarm and materialize
  producers, including `Err(PrewarmSkipped)` batches.
- `drain_specs` checks stable leaves before loading, pool, attach, dirty,
  relayout, or normal reservation effects. A shifted target's uniquely
  identified surviving reservation is settled while a newer replacement index
  remains intact; ambiguous matches are left untouched.
- `handlers::prewarm::resolve_automatic_with` is called by the actual
  `run.rs` automatic worker and puts host rejection both before and after the
  remembered-agent relaunch fold. The executable hostile-environment test uses
  that helper and does not manufacture its own skipped result.
- The broader detached provider/prewarm worker lifecycle and process-tree
  supervision remain pre-existing THE-251-related scope, as required by the
  primary routing instructions; this review does not claim them fixed.
