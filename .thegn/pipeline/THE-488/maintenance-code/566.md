## Scope implemented

Implemented primary revision 4: the ratchet scanner now recognizes ordinary
and byte character literals before string/comment scanning and consumes their
escape-aware bounds. It supports ordinary characters, escaped quotes, escaped
backslashes, simple escapes such as `\\n`, and bounded Unicode escapes such as
`\\u{1f600}`. A quote is treated as a lifetime rather than a character
literal unless a valid closing quote is found within those bounds, preserving
`'static`, `<'a>`, and lifetime bounds such as `'a: 'b`.

The same helper and regressions were mirrored byte-for-byte in:

- `crates/thegn-core/src/test_support/ratchet.rs`
- `crates/thegn-media/src/ratchet.rs`
- `crates/thegn-metrics/src/ratchet.rs`

The existing `profile.rs` allowlist entry remains the explicit, reasoned
reconciliation for the known platform predicate. No `task.rs` pin was added;
its box-drawing characters are comment-only and are handled by the repaired
stripper.

## Regression coverage

- Character literals containing a double quote, escaped quote, escaped
  backslash, simple escape, Unicode escape, and byte character literals remain
  opaque to `code_only`.
